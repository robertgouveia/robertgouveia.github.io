Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Personal Finance App is a personal tool used only by its owner to access the owner's own bank accounts through Enable Banking.

  • Who uses it: only the owner. No other users, no sign-ups.
  • What data: account details, balances and transactions of the owner's own accounts.
  • Where it is stored: only on the owner's own computer. This website stores nothing.
  • Sharing: data is never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Cookies and tracking: this website uses none.
